InnovationLet is an independent technology publication run by the [F9XR Team](https://f9xr.org). This is the Governments desk: regulation, public tech, policy, and procurement.

## What we do

We read the primary sources so you don't have to. Bills, regulations, consultations, and procurement records. Then we write the short version, with links, and without the marketing layer.

## Our standards

- **Primary sources first.** Every factual claim links to where it came from.
- **No vendor copy.** If a measure is framed as reform, we ask what it changes.
- **Say what we don't know.** Uncertainty gets stated, not hidden behind confident phrasing.
