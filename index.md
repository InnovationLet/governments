InnovationLet Governments is the policy desk of the InnovationLet network. We cover how public institutions regulate technology, buy it, and build it themselves. No partisan framing, no press-office language. Just the rules as written, who they bind, and what enforcement actually looks like.

## What we cover

- **Regulation**: data, competition, AI and platform rules, and the bodies that enforce them.
- **Public tech**: digital services, procurement, and the legacy systems government runs on.
- **Policy**: legislation, standards, and the consultations that shape both.
- **Procurement**: contracts, tenders, and where public money actually goes.

## How we report

We follow primary sources first: bills, regulations, consultations, and procurement records. When we cite a clause, we link it. When something is still a proposal, we call it a proposal. The desk publishes daily, with a longer brief each week for subscribers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does InnovationLet Governments cover?**
Regulation, public tech, policy, and procurement. Every story is filed under one of those four areas.

**How often is it updated?**
The desk publishes daily during the week. Subscribe to the newsletter for a weekly round-up.

**Can I pitch a guest post?**
Yes. Send a short pitch to hello@f9xr.org. We review submissions weekly and reply to every one.

**Where can I find the other categories?**
The full network covers artificial intelligence, space, finance, startups, economy, tech, and gaming.
